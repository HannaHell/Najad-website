Our Story

Built in Henaan.
Sailed everywhere.

Najad has been building yachts on the island of Orust since 1971. Over 50 years, one island, one unwavering commitment to quality.

Since 1971

A Swedish yard with a
singular obsession.

Najad was founded in 1971 in Henan, on the island of Orust -- the same island that produces more sailing yachts per square kilometre than anywhere else in the world. From the beginning, the goal was simple: to build the finest bluewater cruising yacht in Sweden.

More than 50 years later, that goal has not changed. Every Najad is still built by hand in Henan, by craftsmen who take pride in every detail. The joinery, the laminate schedule, the rigging layout -- nothing is left to chance.

Najad yachts have circumnavigated the globe, crossed the Atlantic more times than we can count, and found their way into the most demanding harbours in the world. They come back. They always come back.

Our Values

What we stand for.

Passion for Perfection
Our brand promise. Every decision -- from the curve of the hull to the weight of a cabinet door -- is made in pursuit of the best possible outcome. There are no acceptable compromises.
Built to Last
A Najad is not a disposable product. We build yachts that are still sailing 30 years after they left the yard -- and still turning heads in harbours around the world. Quality is not a selling point. It is a necessity.
Your Boat
Every Najad is built to order. We do not maintain a stock of finished yachts waiting for buyers. We build your boat -- to your specification, for the way you sail, for the life you want to live aboard.

The Yard

Henan, Orust.
Since 1971.

Our yard is in Henan, a small town on the island of Orust on the Swedish west coast. Orust is the centre of Swedish boatbuilding -- Hallberg-Rassy, Arcona and OYS are all based here, along with dozens of smaller yards and suppliers.

The concentration of expertise on this island is extraordinary. The craftsmen who build your Najad have often spent their entire careers in the industry. The knowledge runs deep.

Najad AB is part of Najad Group, alongside Arcona Yachts and Orust Yacht Service. The group shares the yard infrastructure and supplier relationships, which means every boat benefits from the combined expertise of all three brands.
