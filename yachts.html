The Range

Six yachts.
One philosophy.

Every Najad is built to order in Henån, Orust. Choose your cockpit configuration -- aft or centre -- and we build the rest around you. No two are exactly alike.

Aft Cockpit

Classic handling. Proven performance.

The aft cockpit places the helm at the stern, giving the helmsman a direct connection to the boat. Traditional, responsive and well-suited to active sailors who want to feel every nuance of the rig.

Privacy, comfort and ocean capability.
