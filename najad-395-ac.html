The 395 AC

Small in size.
Grand in ambition.

The Najad 395 AC is the entry point into the Najad range -- but there is nothing entry-level about it. At 39 feet, she is large enough to cross oceans in comfort and small enough to be handled by two people without drama.

The aft cockpit layout keeps the helmsman connected to the boat. Responsive, direct and a genuine pleasure to sail, the 395 AC rewards good seamanship and forgives the occasional lapse.
