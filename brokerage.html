Why Buy Pre-Owned

A Najad built to
last decades.

Najad yachts are among the most durable production cruising yachts ever built. A well-maintained example from the 1990s or 2000s can still be in outstanding condition -- and still turning heads.

When you buy a pre-owned Najad through us, you get the benefit of our knowledge of the boat and its history. We only list boats we are confident in.
